New in 0.3.5

Spectral film simulation.

Film Print treats the graded image as the light that reaches the film. It exposes each emulsion layer by its spectral sensitivity, develops the layers, and then prints the negative on paper or cinema print stock, scans it, or shows a reversal film on a light table.

How Film Print works (in Chinese)

Photo: Arches National Park (Utah, USA) by LaurentFromSF, discuss.pixls.us, CC BY-SA 4.0. Edited in Alcedo Studio.

  • 30 negatives, 5 reversal films

    Combine any negative with a photographic paper or a cinema print stock, including Cine Look (Kodak Vision 2383). The stock browser filters by process and brand and shows a color swatch for each film.

  • Negative Scan

    A virtual scanner reads and inverts the negative, and OpenDRT forms the SDR or HDR picture. Adjust scan exposure, contrast, cyan, magenta, yellow, and black.

  • Film format and grain

    Choose 16mm, 35mm, or 70mm. Grain forms in the film layers with the granularity data of the stock; smaller formats also enlarge light spread and halation.

New in 0.3.4

Nodes, masks, and AI Masks.

Each Color Grade node has its own adjustments and its own masks, so a grade can apply to the whole frame or only to a region. A mask can be a linear gradient, a radial gradient, or an AI Mask.

  • AI Masks

    Select the subject, background, sky, or people with one click, or any object with points, a drawn path, or a box. Add and subtract selections, and adjust Feather and Expand.

  • Luminance and hue range

    Restrict any mask by brightness and hue, with two-handle bars, softness, and a saturation threshold. Show selection displays the coverage.

  • On your device

    The AI Mask models run locally. Download them in Settings > AI Models, where each model card shows its license and size. Saved masks render without running the models again.

AI agents

Scripts and AI agents.

alcedo-cli installs with Alcedo Studio, and every action in the interface has a command. Claude Code, Codex, or a shell script can open projects, import, rate, edit, search, and export, in the background or in the window you are using.

Get the Alcedo skill for your agent
alcedo-cliSession: your open window
# Backlit and too dark: lift the subject
alcedo-cli --session gui call editor.batch_set '{"changes":[
  {"field":"exposure","value":0.65},
  {"field":"highlights","value":-42},
  {"field":"shadows","value":28}],
  "note":"agent: lift the backlit subject"}'
{"applied":3,…}

  • In your window or in the background

    Share your open window and watch the sliders move, or start a headless session for a folder of photos.

  • Control

    While an agent works, your edit controls are locked, but you can pan, zoom, and read the scopes. Stop ends its control at once.

  • Recorded in the history

    Agent edits are marked in the history with the agent's note, and undo works on them as on your own edits.

LUTs

Film LUTs, and your own.

Browse LUTs by category, source, brand, and print stock; grid tiles preview each LUT on the open photo. Set the encoding a LUT expects and the one it produces, and Alcedo converts between them and the scene space.

Photos: Ryan Breitkreutz (@ryanbreitkreutz), free RAW photos from Signature Edits.

  • 50 encodings

    Scene encodings such as ARRI LogC3 and LogC4, Canon Log, F-Log2 C, V-Log, Apple Log 2, Cineon, and ACES; display encodings such as sRGB, BT.1886, PQ, and HLG. Alcedo can remember the pair for each LUT.

  • Official packages

    113 film LUTs from Spectral Film LUT and spektrafilm, downloaded in Settings > LUTs and signature-checked. Your own .cube files appear under User.

  • Diffusion

    A black mist filter gives bright lights a soft glow and keeps midtones sharp. It sits next to clarity, sharpening, grain, and halation.

Performance

Decoded once, graded live.

RAW decoding and geometry are computed once and cached. Color Grade nodes are not cached: on every change they run again from the cached image on the GPU (CUDA, OpenCL, or Metal), in two buffers used in turn, so GPU memory does not grow with the number of nodes. The preview updates while you drag and settles to full quality when you release.

Scene-referred, from RAW to display.

Alcedo keeps the full range of the RAW file through the edit and forms the picture for your display at the last step, with ACES 2.0, OpenDRT, or Film Print. On a Mac with an HDR display, the editor previews HDR directly.

Workspaces

Library and editor in one window.

The library and the editor share one window and one project file. The file holds the album structure and the full edit history of every photo, so a backup is a copy of one file.

Library

Search by camera, lens, date, ISO, or in natural language with CLIP models that run on your device. Nested collections, star ratings, and edit marks organize the library.
